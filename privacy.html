PRIVACY POLICY

Your files stay yours.

Photos, videos and extracted audio are processed on your device. Here is how the app handles your media, local history, permissions and exports.

Last updated: 21 November 2026 Scope and versions explained below
On-device processing

No upload to an app server is required for conversion.

Your files, your control

Results and operation history are stored locally.

Ad-free release candidate

Build 43 removes Google advertising and consent SDKs.

Who we are and what this policy covers

Media Converter (bundle ID studio.hrlx.mediaconverter) is presented under the HRLX Apps product name. This name identifies the product on this website; it does not by itself establish a separately registered legal company. The legal seller/developer identity is the one shown in the App Store listing once public. Contact hrlxapp@icloud.com for publisher identity or privacy enquiries.

This policy distinguishes the proposed no-ad App Store release (v0.4.25 Build 43) from previous TestFlight builds that included Google's advertising and consent SDKs. Data practices may differ depending on the actual installed build. Build 43 has not been publicly released at the time of this policy update.

Media you choose

You choose images and videos through iOS Photos or Files pickers. The app makes its own working copies and performs image/video conversion and audio extraction locally using Apple's frameworks. Selected media is not uploaded to an HRLX Apps server for conversion. Files you choose to export or share go to the destination you select.

Conversion does not require an HRLX Apps account, contacts, microphone or precise location access. The proposed Build 43 contains no Google advertising SDK; previous TestFlight builds may have contacted Google for test advertisements.

Data stored on the device

App-owned copies of source media, completed output, temporary previews, conversion history, errors and settings may be stored locally. History may contain file names, formats, sizes, dates, statuses and error messages. Settings may include language, typography, automatic saving and local free-tier usage counters.

Media files are stored within the app's iOS sandbox. The app attempts to exclude its media directory from device backups, but this has not been independently verified on all devices and backup paths. Keep separate copies of important exports. Importing does not intentionally modify originals in Photos or Files.

Saving, sharing and Apple services

Saving, sharing and optional automatic saving to Photos use iOS services and permissions. Files exported to Photos, Files, iCloud or other destinations are handled under the settings and policies of those services.

StoreKit 2 supports monthly/yearly subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple supplies localized prices, handles payments, manages subscriptions and provides verified transaction information to determine Pro access. Purchase availability, free-trial eligibility and Sandbox transactions vary by version and Apple Account. We do not receive complete payment-card numbers from StoreKit and do not operate a separate payment server for these purchases. See Apple Privacy.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The proposed App Store release, Media Converter v0.4.25 Build 43, is configured without Google Mobile Ads or Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). It has no in-app advertising or rewarded-ad requests. Local media conversion does not upload your selected files to an HRLX Apps conversion server. No separate third-party usage analytics SDK was identified in the Build 43 source.

Some earlier TestFlight builds included Google Mobile Ads/UMP for test advertising and consent. Those builds may have contacted Google and transmitted device or network data in connection with test ad requests. Google's data practices for those older versions are described in the Google Privacy Policy. If advertising is introduced later, we will update this policy and any required App Store Connect disclosures before enabling it.

Retention and deletion

App-owned files and history can remain until removed from app controls or when the app is deleted, subject to iOS behavior. Deleting a history item instructs the app to remove its associated source and output copies, but file deletion may fail and temporary or recovery files may persist; the current implementation does not verify every deletion was physically completed. Clearing completed sources is intended to keep finished results and history.

Media exported to Photos, Files, iCloud or other destinations must be removed separately there. See Manage Your Data. No HRLX Apps server-side media account is created for ordinary conversion; email sent to support is handled separately below.

Support correspondence and privacy requests

If you contact support, you may voluntarily include your email address, name, device model, iOS/app version, screenshots, diagnostics or attachments. We use messages to respond, troubleshoot, follow up and handle legitimate requests; email providers involved in transport and storage process this correspondence. Avoid sending private media unless necessary.

Support messages are kept as reasonably needed for resolution, follow-up and applicable recordkeeping, and deleted or de-identified when appropriate. No specific verified retention duration is promised. Contact hrlxapp@icloud.com to ask for access, correction or deletion of support data, or to raise a concern. Requests are handled under applicable law and third-party providers may have their own retention rules.

Security

The app's media processing is local and app files are protected by the iOS sandbox; overall security also depends on the device, services used for purchases and support email, and export destinations. No storage or transfer method is completely risk-free.

Children and changes

Media Converter is a general utility not designed to solicit children's personal information. Advertising, if reintroduced in a future version, will require an updated privacy notice and any applicable age and consent review before activation. Changes to material data practices will be reflected in a dated policy update and App Store Connect disclosures where applicable.

This website

This is a static support site. No proprietary HRLX Apps advertising or analytics scripts were found in the reviewed website files. GitHub Pages, as host, may process technical connection information under its own policies. Previous TestFlight builds included Google advertising SDKs; the proposed Build 43 removes them.

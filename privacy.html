PRIVACY POLICY

Your files stay yours.

Photos, videos and extracted audio are processed on your device. Here is how the app handles your media, local history, permissions and exports.

Effective & last updated: 17 November 2026 For the current app build
On-device processing

No upload to an app server is required for conversion.

Your files, your control

Results and operation history are stored locally.

No ad tracking in this build

No third-party advertising or analytics SDK is included.

Who we are and what this policy covers

Media Converter: FormatFlow (displayed on your device as “Media Converter”) is provided by HRLX Apps. This policy describes the app's current implementation, based on version 0.4.8 (Build 21). For privacy questions, contact hrlxapp@icloud.com.

Media you choose

You select images or videos from Apple's Photos picker or Files picker. The app creates working copies on your device to convert formats and extract audio. Conversion happens locally using Apple frameworks. In this version, the app does not upload your selected media to an HRLX Apps server.

We do not require you to create an account. The app does not request access to your contacts, location or microphone for its conversion features.

Data stored on the device

The app stores working source copies, converted results, temporary previews, a local operation history and settings. The history can include the original filename, output format, file size, timestamps, processing status and error messages. Local settings include your preferred language, typography and automatic-save preference. Daily usage information may also be stored locally for feature-limit logic.

App-owned media is stored in the app's sandbox, and the app's media folder is marked to be excluded from device backups. Original files in Photos or Files are not modified when you import them.

Saving, sharing and Apple services

If you choose to export or share a result, the file goes to your selected destination through iOS. When “Automatically save to Photos” is enabled, the app attempts to save eligible completed photos and videos to your photo library, subject to iOS permission. Your device's Photos, Files, sharing services and any iCloud synchronization you enable operate under the relevant Apple or destination-provider policies.

The app includes StoreKit purchase code, but paid purchases are disabled in the current build. If they are enabled in a later release, Apple will manage the payment transaction and purchase entitlements; we will update this policy before changing the app's data practices.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The current app build has no third-party advertising SDK or analytics SDK, and it does not send media or in-app usage analytics to an HRLX Apps backend. We do not use the app to track you across other companies' apps or websites. A future release that adds advertising or analytics will require an updated privacy statement and relevant permissions or disclosures.

Retention and deletion

Local media and operation history remain in the app until you remove them using its controls or remove the app, subject to iOS device behavior. You can clear completed source copies in Settings; this retains completed results and history. Deleting an individual history entry removes its app-owned source and result copies. Deleting completed history can remove completed results. Files you previously saved to Photos or Files must be deleted separately in those destinations.

See Manage Your Data for a practical guide. As the current app has no user account or HRLX Apps media server, there is no online account or server-side media database to request deletion from.

Security

Files are kept within the iOS app sandbox and processed on your device. Device security and any destination you choose also affect protection of your media. No storage method can be described as perfectly secure.

Children and changes

This is a general media utility and does not knowingly ask children for personal information. We may update this policy if app functionality or laws change. A revised date will appear on this page; material changes should be reflected in the app and its disclosures as appropriate.

This website

These pages are static and do not include HRLX Apps analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies. They are intended for hosting on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the website host, may process technical connection data under its own policies, separate from Media Converter's on-device processing.

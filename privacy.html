PRIVACY POLICY

Your files stay yours.

Learn how Media Converter processes, stores and deletes your media and handles optional purchases.

Last updated: 21 November 2026
Converted on your device

Selected media is not sent to our servers for conversion.

Local file management

Your results and conversion history are stored on your device.

No in-app ads

Media Converter does not display advertisements.

Who is responsible for this app?

Media Converter is provided under the HRLX Apps name. For questions about privacy or how your information is handled, contact hrlxapp@icloud.com.

Photos, videos and audio

You choose which files to import from Photos or Files. Media Converter processes them on your device to convert images or videos and extract audio. Your selected media is not uploaded to an HRLX Apps server for conversion, and importing a file does not intentionally change the original.

If you save or share a result to Photos, Files, iCloud or another destination, that destination manages the exported copy according to its own settings and privacy practices.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps working copies, conversion results, temporary previews, and a local history of operations. History can include file names, formats, sizes, dates, processing status and error information. Your language, saving preferences and free usage counters can also be stored on the device.

Files are kept within the app's local storage. Some app data may be affected by your device's backup settings. Keep a separate copy of important results.

Permissions and Apple services

The app accesses only the photos or files you select and uses iOS permissions when saving to Photos. It does not require an HRLX Apps account, your contacts, microphone or precise location for normal conversion.

Optional Pro purchases are handled by Apple. Apple provides the available plans and prices, processes payments and subscription management, and confirms purchase entitlements. We do not receive your full payment card details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising and tracking

Media Converter does not display ads or use advertising services in the version covered by this policy. It does not use third-party advertising trackers or a separate third-party usage analytics service for ordinary conversions.

Keeping and deleting files

Results and history may remain on your device until you remove them using the app or delete the app. Cleaning completed source copies is intended to retain your results. Deleting an operation removes its history entry and attempts to delete the source and result copies held by the app. Some temporary files may remain if cleanup does not complete.

Files you have already exported to Photos, Files, iCloud or another destination must be deleted separately there. See Manage Your Data for instructions.

Messages to support

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, device details, a description of the problem or optional attachments. We use it to respond to your request and keep correspondence as reasonably needed for support and applicable obligations. Email service providers may process those messages. Please avoid sharing private media unless needed.

For questions about your information or requests to access, correct or delete support correspondence, write to hrlxapp@icloud.com.

Security and changes

Your media is processed locally within the protections provided by iOS. No storage or sharing method is completely risk-free. We will update this policy if our data practices change, and show the date of the update.

Visiting this website

This is a static support website. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical connection information under its own privacy practices. The website does not include HRLX Apps advertising or analytics scripts.

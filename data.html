DATA MANAGEMENT

You are in control of your files.

Understand what gets deleted inside Media Converter and what stays in Photos or Files.

Local data, not an online account

Media Converter's current build does not create an HRLX Apps user account or store imported media in an HRLX Apps cloud account. Your app-owned files and history live on your device.

Clear completed source copies

In the app, open Settings → Privacy & Storage → Clear completed source copies. This attempts to remove app-owned source copies from completed tasks while retaining outputs and history. Some file deletion operations can fail; check your app storage afterwards if deletion matters.

This does not delete original photos or files from Apple Photos or Files.

Delete a conversion and its result

Open the results/history section, choose the operation and use its delete control. The app removes the history entry and attempts to delete associated app-owned source and output files. Some file operations may fail without independent physical deletion confirmation. The delete control may be unavailable for an active processing task until it completes or is cancelled.

Delete saved copies and uninstall

If you saved or shared an export to Photos, Files or another destination, delete that exported copy separately using that destination's controls. Removing the app using iOS normally removes app-owned local data; it does not remove copies already saved outside the app.

Need assistance?

Because no HRLX Apps account or media server is involved in the current build, there is no online media account to close. For a question about local deletion, support correspondence or data-rights requests, email hrlxapp@icloud.com.

DATA MANAGEMENT

You are in control of your files.

Understand what gets deleted inside Media Converter and what stays in Photos or Files.

Where your files are stored

Media Converter keeps imported working copies, conversion results and history on your device. You do not need to create an account to convert files.

Clear completed source copies

In the app, open Settings → Privacy & Storage → Clear completed source copies. This attempts to free space by removing source copies from finished tasks while keeping results and history. Some cleanup operations may not finish successfully.

Your original photos and files in Photos or Files are not deleted by this action.

Delete an operation and its result

Open the results or history area, choose an operation and use Delete. The app removes its record and attempts to delete source and output copies stored in the app. An active operation may need to finish or be cancelled first.

Files you exported

Copies already saved to Photos, Files, iCloud or another destination must be deleted separately there. Removing the app usually deletes its local data, but not copies saved elsewhere.

Questions about your data?

For help with deletion or questions about information you sent to support, email hrlxapp@icloud.com.

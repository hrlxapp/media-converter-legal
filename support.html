Frequently asked questions

Which conversions does the app support?

Image exports include JPEG, PNG and HEIC; video exports include MP4 and MOV when supported by the device; audio extraction creates M4A/AAC when the source contains a compatible audio track. Compatibility may vary with source codec and iOS capabilities.

Does the app upload my photos and videos?

No. The current conversion pipeline processes selected files on your device and does not send the media to an HRLX Apps server. You choose whether to save or share a result.

Where are my converted files?

Completed results are kept in the app until removed. Open your results/history and tap a result to preview it or use the save/share controls. Files you export to Photos or Files are managed in those destinations.

Why can't I export a video?

The source file or codec may not be supported, the device may need more free space, or the video may not contain a compatible audio track. Try a shorter file, verify available storage and check the error shown in the app.

Are there ads or paid plans?

Media Converter does not display ads. You can use the free daily conversions or choose Pro for additional flexibility. Monthly, yearly and lifetime options may be offered through Apple. The app shows available plans, local prices and renewal terms before purchase.

How do I remove stored files?

See Manage Your Data. Clearing completed source copies is different from deleting a result. Exported files in Photos/Files are not deleted by clearing app history.
